The Maze of Magna

Build your labyrinth. Command your heroes. Hold the line.
◆

In the realm of Magna, ancient forces stir beneath the eastern mountains. Undead hordes pour endlessly from the Ashen Wastes, drawn onward by a dark signal none can yet name — a pulse felt in bone and stone alike. Villages fall silent. Roads grow cold. And still they come.

Only you stand between the tide and the kingdom's heart. Raise your walls, command your champions, and let the labyrinth do what blades alone cannot. Every corridor you build is a trap. Every hero you place is a last line of defiance. The maze is your weapon. Use it well.
